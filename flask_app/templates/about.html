Research & Technology

Deploying state-of-the-art Deep Learning models to provide rapid, accessible agronomy diagnostics for sustainable agriculture.

Project Overview

CropGuard AI is a state-of-the-art smart agriculture utility redesigned to provide real-time identification of crop leaf infections. By bridging deep learning diagnostics with conversational artificial intelligence, the application aids research efforts and field agronomy education.

Designed around two deep convolutional neural network architectures—optimized for local edge and high-performance cloud processing respectively—the platform classifies 27 diverse healthy and pathological categories across six major crops (Apple, Corn, Grape, Potato, Strawberry, Tomato). It integrates seamlessly with Google's Gemini LLM to yield instantaneous, eco-friendly treatment suggestions.

Technical Architecture

CropGuard AI operates on a dual-model framework, utilizing optimized deep transfer networks pretrained on standard datasets:

The **MobileNet-V2** model is highly compressed and tailored for fast client-side/edge execution, using inverted residual blocks and depthwise separable convolutions to classify leaf scans at **93.54% test accuracy**.

The **EfficientNet-B3** model is scaled compoundly to assess rich geometric and color patterns on high-performance cloud hardware. Featuring dense transfer layers and batch normalization, it delivers an exceptional test classification accuracy of **96.56%**.
